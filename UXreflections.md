Our Landing/Homepage contains a search bar, navigation bar, and 10 buttons. The buttons take you to the class list, and each button filters by a certain category.

Our class list contains a nav bar, filter bar, and a list of all the classes. In the list you can also see a summary of each class, the reported difficulty, and the reported workload. When you click on a class, you are brought to the designated page for that class.

Each class page has a section for information on the class and a section for reviews of the class. The information portion of the page contains a more in depth description of the class, the prerequisites, the subject, reported difficulty, reported workload, documents submitted by the teacher, and the teachers who teach that class. The reviews portion of the page shows all reviews of that class and a button for you to add your own review. Reviews can not be about teachers and can only be about the class's rigor and workload.
