**Must haves:**
- All courses listed (We will retrieve the course list from the CCA website)
- Filter system (To improve user interactivity, the filter system will allow users to pick classes based on certain preferences)
- Categories (All courses will be scored on topics such as rigor, workload, content, etc.)
- Input from teachers/students (Teachers and students will be able to leave comments/reviews on classes)
- Class Description (Each individual class will have a description that is unique to it)

**Should have:**
- Search Bar (Users can directly search for details of a specific class)
- Profile (Required to sign in school email)
- Course recommendation based on profile (Every will be able to set their own preferences)
- Syllabus (In addition to class description, participating teachers may upload their syllabus)
- Requirement to mention what teacher they had (will be noted on MVP but not required)

**Could have:**
- Map of where the room is (May or may not be interactive based on chosen classes)
- Lofi music (Cool addition that may be soothing for users)
- Reviewers apply to become official reviewers (Admins will star official reviewers and their reviews will be the first reviews that all users see. Official reviews must be vivid and informational)
- Review by moderator (Moderators will be able to remove any reviews or comments that are inappropriate. Moderators are NOT the developers and are third parties. The developers will be classified as admins by default.)
- Dark mode (To reduce eye strain in environments with low light, dark mode may be toggled.)

**Won't have:**
- RATING TEACHERS (Rating teachers is inappropriate and will not be tolerated)
- Uploaded profile pictures (Users can only choose profile pictures that are created by the developers)
- Ads (Users will not be forced to view any ads)
- Subscription system (Users will not be given the opportunity to pay for anything)
- NFTs and stocks (Self explanatory)
