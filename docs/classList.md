| Peer Assistant Listeners (PALs) | Application or Audition Only | Managers must enroll for both terms to be eligible. Application-only for general members | none |  | The mission statement for the Peer Assistance Leaders (PALs) is “PALs promote student wellness and encourage a safe and positive school community.” In the pursuit of this goal, the course offers an introduction to peer counseling, project design and management, public speaking and leadership, financial management, fundraising, and publicity. PALs are expected to learn the fundamentals of active listening used by professional counselors including, perception checking, clarifying, acknowledgement, summarizing, and mirroring. Each student in PALs receives a six hour training from credentialed members of the counseling staff and PALs advisor. Prior to actually counseling another student, the PALs will compile background on the student, formulate a possible strategy to help, and plan follow-up meetings. PALs are expected to create projects with a focus on improving school wellness. |
