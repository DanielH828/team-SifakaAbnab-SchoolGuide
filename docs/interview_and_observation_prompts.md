**Observation:**

Prompt 1: The student is given a random class that they have not taken and are asked to research the class to a level where they are fully comfortable deciding whether or not they would take the class.

Prompt 2: Ask the student to find prerequisites of a math class they have not yet taken (or another type of information).

Prompt 3: Construct a valid fictional schedule for a rising 10th-grade student who has previously taken Freshman Dance PE, IM1, and IM2H, English 9, Freshman Bio, Spanish 2 and 3, and non-western World History. They are interested in joining the Humanities conservatory, joining ASB, and starting/completing the Computer Science CTE Pathway. They have received a grade of D in IM2H and wish to take Digital Electronics. 
*The observer should record the amount of time it takes. 

**Interview:**
1. Walk me through how you chose classes for next year.
2. Have you ever felt conflicted about choosing classes? Or were unable to choose between classes? Describe the experience?
3. Have you ever spoken to the counselor about your class schedule, either initiated by you or your counselor? Describe the experience and why a discussion took place.
4. After having taken the classes that you have this year, would you change anything about how you chose your class schedule?
5. Is there any information you would have liked to have when choosing your schedule that you could not find? If so, what?
